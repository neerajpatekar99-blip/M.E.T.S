Standard 1.0 mm precision-ground stainless steel 316L disc placed directly over the soft lithium metal chip. Standardized coin cell protocol specifies this single heavy planar spacer to fill the internal cavity with uniform mechanical stack pressure without requiring a wave spring.
Laboratory SOP Protocol In glovebox benchtop fabrication, omitting the wave spring eliminates spring slippage, avoids can-rim short circuits, and prevents uneven point-stress gouges into soft lithium metal foil.
